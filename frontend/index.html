About AI Resume Analyzer

AI Resume Analyzer is an intelligent web application designed to help students and job seekers optimize their resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). Upload your resume, receive an ATS score, analyze your skills, identify missing keywords, and get personalized recommendations to improve your chances of getting shortlisted.
